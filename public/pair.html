Nexus Pair

Link your WhatsApp and get your session ID, delivered straight to your own chat.

----
  1. Open WhatsApp > Settings > Linked devices
  2. Tap Link a device, then Link with phone number instead
  3. Enter the code above
  4. Your session ID arrives in your own chat ("Message yourself")

Never share your session ID. Anyone who has it controls your WhatsApp account. Put it in your host as SESSION_ID.
